# Six-seat operating agreement
Astra schedules its two owner-side workers directly. Only the six seats may own tickets. Owner-side Neon Extra High may delegate a bounded task within its ticket to Hydrogen; Hydrogen has no separate ticket, branch or lane and may not delegate. The friend’s three instances are peers: negotiate dependencies, do not spawn or command extra workers. Two distinct Colab sessions, no shared credentials. No seventh coordinator, reviewer or runtime seat. If a seat is unavailable, pause its lane or reassign to an existing seat. Development hosts may use hosted sessions with synthetic fixtures only. None of these hosts, credentials or services is a product dependency.

Before allocating critical work, give each seat identical sealed synthetic tasks: repair a schema violation, implement a delayed wait, and review a consent bug. Use isolated copies, same inputs, fixed 30-minute cap and two seeds. Blind cross-score correctness (6 points: passing cases), privacy/offline invariants (2), reproducibility (2); require 8/10 and no invariant breach. Record seat/configuration, resource limits, time, tests, retries and reviewer. Compare both Neon and OSS instances separately; rotate failed lanes and rerun. Do not infer strengths from aliases. No scores exist yet.

Single new repository, no forks. Local branches `lane/<lane>/<ticket>` from the same baseline; separate worktrees. Astra owns shared contracts, integration branch and release checklist. One writer per lane. Start each ticket with owner, reviewer, paths, dependency, acceptance check. At each integration checkpoint post baseline hash, changed paths, reproducible test, observed output, blockers and next consumer. For breaking schema changes open a versioned proposal, obtain both producer and consumer signoff, land compatibility fixtures first. Merge only after independent review, synthetic provenance and tests; revert failed integrations rather than editing another active lane. Never force-push. No remotes, uploads or publication in this planning task; later one public upstream with collaborators only after explicit owner approval and clean history review.
